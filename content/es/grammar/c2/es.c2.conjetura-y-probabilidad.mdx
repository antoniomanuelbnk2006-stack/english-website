## In one sentence

Spanish has several ways to guess, to estimate and to hedge: the **future** and the **conditional** express conjecture, **quizá / tal vez / acaso** introduce doubt, and **es probable que / puede que** frame a possibility.

## Form

| Structure | Mood | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Future = guess about the present | indicative | *Estará en casa.* (He must be at home.) |
| Conditional = guess about the past | indicative | *Tendría veinte años.* (He was probably twenty.) |
| *Quizá, tal vez, acaso* + verb | subjunctive (or indicative if the speaker is fairly sure) | *Quizá tengas razón.* |
| *Es probable / posible que, puede que* | subjunctive | *Es probable que ocurra lo contrario.* |
| *Probablemente, seguramente* | indicative or subjunctive | *Seguramente llegará tarde.* |
| *Deber de* + infinitive | — | *Debe de estar enfermo.* |

- *Seguro que* and *a lo mejor* are followed by the **indicative**: *Seguro que viene. / A lo mejor llueve.*
- *Dudar que* and *no creer que* take the subjunctive; *creer que* and *suponer que* take the indicative.

## Use

- **Everyday guesses:** *¿Qué hora será? — Serán las cinco.*
- **Narrative guesses about the past:** *Entonces, el pueblo tendría unos mil habitantes.*
- **Degrees of certainty** from strongest to weakest: *seguro que + indicative* → *probablemente* → *es probable que + subjunctive* → *quizá + subjunctive* → *no creo que + subjunctive*.
- **In argumentation**, hedging with the conditional and *quizá* gives a cautious, academic tone: *Quizá convenga mirar la traducción desde el lado del lector.*

## Contrast with English

- English expresses guesses with modal verbs (*must be, might, would have been*); Spanish uses verb tenses (the future and the conditional) as well as adverbs.
- *Deber* + infinitive is obligation (*debes descansar*), but *deber de* + infinitive is probability (*debe de estar cansado*); in modern usage the two are often mixed.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ *Quizá tienes razón.* (acceptable, but weaker) → ✓ *Quizá **tengas** razón.* when you present it as a possibility.
- ✗ *Es probable que viene.* → ✓ *Es probable que **venga**.*
- ✗ *A lo mejor venga.* → ✓ *A lo mejor **viene**.*
- ✗ *Serían las cinco* for a present guess → ✓ *Serán las cinco.*

## How it sounds

Guesses often come with a rising or hesitant intonation and a slightly slower rhythm: *Serán… las cinco, ¿no?* Native speakers also use short markers such as *pues*, *a ver* and *digo yo* to soften the claim.
